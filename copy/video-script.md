# FlockCast: demo video script (60–90 s)

About 190 words spoken ≈ 75–80 s at a calm pace. Two speakers: **A** and **B** (swap names in as you like).
Before recording: open http://127.0.0.1:8000/app/ full-screen, scrolled to the top, step 1 selected.

---

## Part 1: On camera, faces (0:00–0:25)

**A:** San Francisco has about 400 Flock cameras reading license plates. California law says those reads can go to California police, and no one else.

**B:** But SFPD's own audit found 299 searches run for federal and out-of-state agencies. And for about six months, Flock left SF's data open to police nationwide. SFPD says it never authorized that.

**A:** In February, the Board of Supervisors votes on extending Flock's contract. So we built FlockCast, to show where one plate read actually goes.

---

## Part 2: Screen only (0:25–1:20)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:25 | Top of page, stat cards | **B:** These numbers come straight from public sources. SFPD shares with 307 agencies, all in California, which is what the law requires. But 299 searches went to federal and out-of-state agencies anyway. |
| 0:38 | Click **Step 1** | **A:** Here's the journey of one plate read. Step one: the camera uploads it to Flock's cloud. Every step shows the exact rule, quoted from the law or the policy, with a link to the source. |
| 0:50 | Click **Step 3**, then **Step 4** | **B:** Step three: SFPD shares access with a regional fusion center that's supposed to serve only California police. Step four: out-of-state agencies got in anyway. On the right is what actually happened, from audits, news reports and court filings. |
| 1:03 | Scroll to the **district map**, hover one district | **A:** This map counts the cameras in each supervisor district, so you can see your own neighborhood and your supervisor. Totals only: we never show exact camera locations. |
| 1:13 | Scroll to **One honest limit** | **B:** One limit: we only see what was published or audited. Anything nobody audited stays invisible. |
| 1:20 | Back to top | **A:** Every claim is sourced. Click it and check. That's FlockCast. |

---

### Notes
- **Say only these numbers:** about 400 cameras · 299 searches · about six months · 307 agencies · February vote. Don't say the 1.6 million figure unless you add "the SF Standard counted".
- Say "possible conflict" or "concern", never "violation" or "illegal".
- **Too long?** Cut the Step 4 sentence and the district-map hover. That brings it to about 65 s.
- **Room to spare?** At 0:50 add: "SFPD's audit says those searches were run by analysts at another agency, through the fusion center's access."
