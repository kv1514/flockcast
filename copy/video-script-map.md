# FlockCast video script (about 85 seconds)

Part 1 is on camera (faces). Part 2 is a screen recording of the live site, with your voices over it.
The site's autoplay sets the pace of Part 2: each stop takes about 9 seconds, so read each block when the map lands there.

Before recording Part 2: open https://kv1514.github.io/flockcast/ in a full-screen browser, wait for the San Francisco map, and don't touch anything except the play button.

---

## Part 1: faces (about 25 s)

**You:** San Francisco has about 400 Flock cameras reading license plates across the city.

**Helen:** California law says those reads can go to California police, and no one else. No federal agencies, no police in other states.

**You:** But SFPD's own audit found 299 searches run for federal and out-of-state agencies.

**Helen:** In February, the Board of Supervisors votes on extending Flock's contract. So we asked a simple question: where does one plate read actually go?

---

## Part 2: screen recording, voice-over (about 60 s)

| Time after recording starts | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | San Francisco map, shaded by cameras per district | **You:** This is FlockCast. The shading shows cameras per district; we never show exact locations. Let's follow one read. *(Click ▶ Follow the read as you finish.)* |
| 0:05 | Plate flies to Atlanta, zooms in on Flock Safety | **Helen:** First stop: Flock's cloud, a private company in Atlanta. Is it allowed to hold your plate? Yes, but only as SFPD's host. |
| 0:14 | Flies back to SF, zooms in on SFPD headquarters | **You:** Next, SFPD. Officers can search it for the uses their policy lists, with a case number or a reason. The record already shows a possible conflict. |
| 0:23 | Short hop to NCRIC | **Helen:** Then NCRIC, a regional fusion center that should serve California police only. Analysts used its access to run those 299 searches. |
| 0:32 | Lines fan out across the country | **You:** Police in other states: not allowed under state law. For about six months, Flock left SF's data open to them anyway. |
| 0:41 | Zooms in on Washington, DC | **Helen:** Federal agencies: also not allowed. According to SFPD, they included the DEA, the IRS and the US Marshals. |
| 0:47 | Whole path, coast to coast | **You:** Five stops. Every one has a documented problem, and every claim links to its source. |
| 0:53 | (hold on the full path) | **Helen:** Our one limit: we only see what was audited or published. That's FlockCast. |

---

### Notes
- If you fall behind the map, skip the second sentence of a block; never speed up.
- Say "possible conflict", never "violation" or "illegal".
- Only these numbers: about 400 cameras, 299 searches, about six months, February vote.
- Want it shorter? Drop the 0:53 line and end on "every claim links to its source" (about 75 s total).
